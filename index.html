Tapli

tapliapp.com ↗

QR ordering for restaurants, live in Finland with real orders going through it daily. A guest scans the code at the table, orders and pays on their phone, and the ticket hits the kitchen display instantly — no app to download, no waiting around to catch someone's eye.

I build and ship it myself, AI-assisted: restaurant dashboard, customer menu, table-side ordering, and kitchen display. TypeScript and React on Supabase and Cloudflare, with Stripe Connect Express so restaurants are paid directly and VAT receipts and refunds are handled automatically.

The interesting problems are on the import side: point AI at a website URL, a PDF, or a photo of a paper menu and it extracts every dish, price, and category in seconds, then tags all 14 EU allergens and translates the whole thing into 20+ languages in one click.

Perch

source ↗

A native macOS menu bar utility bundling window tiling, a screenshot tool, an Alt-Tab switcher, clipboard history, system monitoring, disk and screen cleaning, keep-awake sessions and per-display brightness — the things I kept installing six separate apps for.

Written in Swift and built without Xcode: SwiftPM, lipo for a universal binary, and self-tests that live inside the shipped binary. It updates itself in place, refusing any build not signed by the same key as the one running.

Transcriber

source ↗

Paste a TikTok, Reels, or YouTube link and get the transcript back — the whole video or any slice of it, with or without timestamps. Everything runs locally: yt-dlp pulls the audio, Whisper transcribes it, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Python, with one shared core behind both a command line tool and a small Flask interface that streams each line in as it is recognised. Runs at 1.8x realtime on an M4, exports plain text or SRT, and I reach it from my phone over Tailscale rather than exposing it to the internet.

Company

Tapli

tapliapp.com ↗

A SaaS product for QR ordering in restaurants, live in Finland with real orders going through it every day. Most restaurants are up and running in under 30 minutes.

A guest scans a sticker or a coaster at the table, or picks their table off the floor plan. They order and pay on their phone — Apple Pay, Google Pay, MobilePay, or card — and it lands on the kitchen display instantly, routed to the right station. No app, and no waiting around to catch someone's eye.

Setup is the part restaurants expect to hate. Paste your website URL, upload a PDF, or photograph your paper menu, and AI extracts every item, price, and category in seconds. You review it before anything saves. All 14 EU allergens get tagged, customers filter in real time, and one click translates the whole menu into 20+ languages.

Past the menu itself: daily specials that auto-expire, live order tracking, scan and search analytics exportable as CSV, brand styling with live preview, and AI-drafted replies to Google reviews. It also runs for food trucks, festival stalls, and hotels — room service, bar, and spa through one QR code.

I started Tapli and run it day to day. Sales, marketing, growth, pricing, and deciding with our CTO what we build and what we leave alone. Restaurants will ask you for everything, and most of the job is working out which of it actually moves the business. I still sell and onboard most accounts myself, which is where the roadmap comes from.
